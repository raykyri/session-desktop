Rows, not nesting. A child is an item in the same list as its question, with the same hit area, hover and selection. ↑ ↓ move through questions and children alike; ← goes to the parent (or collapses it), → expands.
What stays with the parent. Children aren't dragged or filed on their own; dragging the question moves its children with it. Each child has its own star button to unstar it, with Undo.
Variants. Outline relies on indent alone. Rail and Elbows draw the hierarchy. Grouped removes the rule between a question and its children so the group reads as one block, with tighter child rows.
