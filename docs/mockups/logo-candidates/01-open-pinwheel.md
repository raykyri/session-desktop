# 01 Open pinwheel

The reference's eight looped petals reduced to their leading edges: eight tapered crescents, each a single open stroke.
Each blade's centerline runs from r=60 to r=240 (viewBox 512) while turning 100°, with angle eased by t^1.5 so the curl tightens toward the tip.
Width follows t^0.9·(1−t)^1.6 (hairline at the root, widest about a third of the way out, point at the tip), max 34.
Color runs along each blade: pale mint at the root, green, cyan, then violet at 15% opacity at the tip, with a 7px Gaussian glow.
Parameters to tweak: blade count, sweep angle, inner radius (size of the hollow center), width exponents.
