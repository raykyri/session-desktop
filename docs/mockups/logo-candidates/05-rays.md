**05 Rays.** Five open pinwheel blades, each a pair of parallel rays (a long leading ray and a shorter one trailing it), like the vertical striations of an aurora curtain, around an empty disc of radius 82. Color and mono use identical paths; color adds one gradient per ray (mint at the base, through cyan, to violet at the tip) and a faint blurred copy (stdDeviation 8, 40% opacity).
Geometry: each ray follows a polar curve where r runs linearly from `R_IN`=82 to `R_IN + (R_OUT-R_IN)*frac` (`R_OUT`=226) and theta = blade angle + ray offset + `SWIRL`*u^`BEND` (55°, 1.6, u = normalized radius). Rays leave the center radially and hook in the rotation direction; theta depends on absolute radius, so the two rays in a blade stay parallel.
Rays are filled polygons offset from the curve. Width tapers from the base width to `TIP`=0.3× at a rounded tip, and the base is cut along the r=82 circle so the center stays round.
Parameters: `BLADES`=5 and per-ray `(offset°, length frac, base width)` = (0, 1.00, 28) and (−21, 0.72, 21). A 3-ray variant (offsets 0/−15/−30°, widths 16/14/12) reads well at 512px but its rays merge at 32px.
For the ASCII background: glyph density can be the distance to these curves, attenuated by u.
