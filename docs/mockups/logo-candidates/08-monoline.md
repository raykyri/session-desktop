# 08 Monoline (new in round 2)

Four constant-width spiral strokes (30 px wide, round caps), r 80→232 with a 104° sweep and angle eased by t^1.4.
Being monoline, it can be drawn with a single stroke weight, so it suits line-icon sets and favicon hinting.
Color is the amber-to-coral app-icon gradient, extended into magenta at the tips. The mono version is the same geometry with currentColor.
Parameters to tweak: stroke width, sweep and the inner radius.
