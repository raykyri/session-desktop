Summary

The proposal turns the research view into one horizontal strip of columns: the feed, then the open conversation as a pair of columns, then a Branches column listing the conversation's branches, which becomes the next pair when one of them is selected. Questions in the feed can be filed into Drafts, Archive, and user folders by dragging. A conversation is a messages column (the user's messages, with the ask box at the end) next to an answer column that shows the answer to the selected message. A branch opens from a passage in the answer, from a marker in the answer's right margin, or from its row in the Branches column.

Every level, root or branch, is the same pair, so going deeper adds columns and never changes the width of the ones already open; the strip scrolls sideways when they don't fit. Rows are quiet: no fill or separators at rest, an inset rounded bubble on hover and selection, and a faint accent ring on the row that has keyboard focus. The left sidebar can collapse to a 52px icon strip; the traffic lights and the sidebar toggle beside them keep the same reserved zone with either sidebar.

Review of the current app

Findings from the screenshots and the code (App.tsx, ResearchColumns.tsx, ResearchDocument.tsx, shell.css, tauri.conf.json).

  • You can't read a branch next to its parent. Branches are cards in a 220–260px rail beside the answer. Opening one replaces the document with the branch's own spine, plus a Back link. To go two levels deep you have to follow Back links, and nothing shows where you are in the tree.
  • The layout has only two panes. ResearchColumns splits the window into a feed column and a content column, and below 880px it shows one. Nothing gives a third column room.
  • Long threads read as one tall scroll. Collapsing works on the whole transcript ("Show full transcript"), not per turn. In a four-turn thread like the Richard Ngo summary, the questions end up screens apart, and there is no overview of the turns.
  • Questions can't be filed. Client-side folders were removed in d51a8d4, although the backend storage for them was kept. Archive is a separate view, not a place you can drop a question. In the first screenshot, a post titled "Drafts" is being used as a folder, which shows the need.
  • Feed cards repeat their controls. Every card shows both a Follow text button and a bookmark, so the controls compete with the titles. Follow has no behavior yet beyond storing a flag (set_research_tree_followed).
  • A collapsed sidebar can strand you. Collapsing sets its width to 0. On Home, Bookmarks, and Archived no button brings it back; only ⇧⌘G and the command palette do.
  • The traffic lights overlap content. The window uses the Overlay title bar without setting trafficLightPosition. With the sidebar collapsed, the lights cover the document header's back and forward buttons, and there is no drag region outside the sidebar. Fullscreen isn't handled, so the 32px top padding stays even when macOS hides the lights.
  • The sidebar toggle moves. It sits at the right edge of a resizable sidebar (208–420px), so its position depends on the sidebar's width. After you collapse the sidebar, the button you just clicked is somewhere else.

The column model

The strip holds the feed, then one [messages | answer] pair per level: level 0 is the root conversation, level 1 the branch selected in the Branches column after it, and so on. A note is a note column and a follow-ups column instead, and its next level is one of its follow-ups. A conversation is a chain of turns, which maps to inlineChainFor(node) in today's code. A branch is a non-inline child node with a queryAnchor.

Rules

  • Every level is the same pair. The messages column lists the conversation's messages in full (clamped to 12 lines, with Show more) and ends with the ask box. The answer column shows only the answer to the selected message, with no composer. Each column keeps its own selection: selecting another message replaces the answer beside it and leaves the columns to its right as they are, since what they list doesn't depend on it (researchColumns.ts).
  • The Branches column. After a level whose messages have branches, a Branches column lists every branch of every message that level lists, one group per branch in reading order: the source message and passage, the branch's messages, and its queue. With nothing selected it stands alone at the messages width. Selecting a row in it (or a branched passage, a margin marker, or a row's branch indicator) makes it the next level's messages column: its header spans the answer column beside it, the selected group ends with the ask box, and its × ("Clear branch selection") closes the columns after it.
  • Margin markers. The answer column keeps a narrow right margin. A paragraph with branched passages gets a link-styled marker there, level with its first line: an up-right arrow and the number of branches, or the running or failed glyph while a message in them runs or has failed. Whole-answer branches get a marker at the end of the answer. Hovering or focusing a marker shows the branch questions in a popover, each marked Open, Running or Failed. The open branch's marker is accent and underlined and shows no popover, and clicking it clears the next column's selection. A paragraph with several branches has one marker, which opens the first.
  • The parent shows where you are. Passages with branches are tinted blue; the passage of the open branch is also underlined. Highlights are tinted amber.
  • One header bar per pair. A level's messages and answer columns share one header bar. The root's has Back and Forward, the conversation title (clamped to 4 lines), "Archived" or "Imported conversation" when they apply, and a … button ("Thread actions") with the first message's answer menu; the Branches column's reads "Branches · N". The pair that holds keyboard focus has full-strength header text; the others are one step dimmer. Header cells across the strip share the height of the tallest, so the band reads as one row.
  • Widths depend only on the window, until resized. They are set from the strip's width: the window less its borders and the sidebar or icon strip. Opening, closing or switching a branch never resizes a column. A handle on each column's right edge sets the width of every column of its kind (feed, messages, answer); a double-click restores the automatic width. When the pairs don't fit, the strip scrolls sideways.
  • Starting a branch. Selecting text in a finished answer offers Branch, Highlight and Copy. Branch, or typing a character, adds an unsent branch to the Branches column, placed by its passage among the branches of the same message, with the passage and an ask box labelled "New branch"; the branch is saved when its first question is sent. Esc or the × cancels it. "Branch from the whole answer" is in the answer's … menu, and ⇧⌘↵ in an ask box sends its question as a branch from the column's last finished answer.
  • Highlights work as before. They stay as character offsets within one block, as with ResearchHighlightAnchor today. A selection has to stay within one paragraph. Clicking a highlight opens a small menu with Branch from highlight, which starts a new branch on that passage, and Remove highlight.

Costs

  • Each branch level adds two columns. At 1440px one pair fits beside the feed; a second level scrolls the feed and the root out of view.
  • The margin takes about 16px of reading width from the answer.
  • A marker shows only an icon and a count, so the branch questions are read one paragraph at a time, on hover or focus, rather than as a list.
  • The answer to only one message per level is visible at a time; reading two answers of the same conversation side by side isn't possible.

Chrome

  • Pair header: Back and Forward, the conversation title, then the … button with the first message's answer menu; no Ask button and no "Answer" label. ⌘J focuses the current level's ask box, and holding ⌘ shows "⌘J" on it. Move to is in the question's … menu in the feed, and below the answer's own actions in the root pair's answer menus.
  • Source: each group of the Branches column starts with its source message on one line and the passage in italics, clamped to two lines.
  • Message rows have no time, star or status pill. A 14px glyph before the first line shows a queued, running, failed or stopped message, and a meta line names Queued or Stopped. A message whose answer has branches has an up-right arrow at the end of its first line (accent while one of them is selected, the running or failed glyph while one runs or failed, a dot when one has a new answer). The … menu and, with two or more messages, a drag handle for reordering sit in a pill in the corner on hover. The … menu starts with the answer's token count, run time and model, then "Branch from the whole answer" and "Copy answer".
  • Answer footer is the time (and " · Edited" for an edited answer), under a rule. A completed answer can start with its recap.
  • Rows in the feed and the messages columns have no fill or separators at rest. Hover shows an inset rounded bubble at 55% of the selected fill; the selected row is a bubble with the selected fill; the row that keys act on shows a 1px accent ring at 35% opacity. A focused answer column shows the same ring inside the column, below the header.
  • Sidebar has no counts next to Home, Highlights or folders.
  • Feed composer starts Home; in a folder or Drafts it ends the list, and Archive and the Trash have none. Once the field is focused or holds text, a row under it shows the Note | Ask switch (Note first), a … button with Save draft (only with text), and Send. In Note mode the placeholder reads "Write or paste a link" and Send saves a note, or a link when the text is a single URL.

Costs

  • The number of highlights is only visible after opening the view.

Feed cards

  • Row glyph. Every card starts with a 14px glyph for its kind and state: a ring for a question (check when answered, a centre dot when updated since last viewed, a pulsing half pie while running, "!" when failed, × when stopped, dashed when queued), a rounded square for a note or a link, a page for a document, a dashed square for a draft. Named states are announced with the card.
  • Text. A title (13.5px, weight 600) shows above the question when it differs; the question under it is muted and clamped to two lines. Without a title the question is 14px in the text colour. Notes, and questions still waiting for a title, show at most four muted lines; a note's Markdown heading line takes the title's style. A link post shows its URL and host in a card. URLs in card text take the link colour. There is no metadata row.
  • Composer. No keyboard hint text. Enter sends when the field has text; Shift+Enter adds a line; Esc leaves the field. The send button is disabled while the field is empty.
  • One pill. A row's controls are one pill in its top-right corner, over the end of the text: a group's chevron, the drag handle, and "…". It shows on hover, while its menu is open, and while focus is inside it. "…" opens Make child of…, Move out of group (children), Collapse or Expand (parents), Move to with New folder…, Rename, and Delete (D). A draft's menu is Open and Delete. A second click on "…" closes it.

Conversation turns

  • Messages on the left, the selected answer on the right. Messages are shown in full, as rows in the messages column. Clicking one shows its answer in the answer column; nothing is sticky.
  • Answers are shown in full. There is no clamp, fade or expand control; the answer column scrolls.
  • Turn actions. A message's … menu holds the answer's details, "Branch from the whole answer" and "Copy answer". Its branch indicator opens the first of its branches, or clears the open one. Selecting text in a finished answer gives Branch, Highlight and Copy. Clicking a branched passage opens its branch. A running answer's status line has Show trace and Stop ("Stopping…" until it stops).
  • Failed answers. The error box is followed by Retry and Edit question. Retry while another answer in the conversation is running queues the retry behind it; removing it from the queue brings the failed attempt back. Edit question puts the failed question in the ask box, labelled "Editing", with a × (or Esc) to cancel; sending replaces the failed turn.
  • The ask box belongs to its column. Each messages column ends with its own ask box, the equivalent of today's "Continue thread", so you can ask in a parent without leaving the branch. It sends with ⌘↵; ↵ adds a line, and Send shows "⌘↵" while it would send. A question asked while an answer is running ("Queue a follow-up") is held in the chain's queue, listed after its messages with Cancel, and sent when the answer finishes. After a stopped or failed answer the box is disabled, with a note above it, until the answer is run again or its question edited. A draft's ask box gives the text the full width with Send on its own row, and opening a draft never focuses it.

Folders and dragging

  • Where folders appear. The Home view starts with the composer, lists unfiled questions without a heading, then a tray for each folder (Drafts, user folders, then Archive), the Trash tray (collapsed, takes no drops) and a "+ New folder" link. The same folders are in the sidebar, or as icons in the strip. "Home" is the view and "Unfiled" is the place: a question is never "in Home". In a tray header, the folder name opens the folder; the rest of the header and the chevron collapse or expand the tray.
  • Drop targets. Dragging uses pointer events: a press anywhere on a card starts it after 5px, and a two-line label of the card follows the pointer. You can drop a question on a tray, on a sidebar row, or on a strip icon (its label shows while you hover during a drag). The Home row is a drop target for Unfiled, so a question can go back from any view. Dropping between cards in a tray sets the order, shown by an insertion line. The middle band of a card that can hold children nests the question under it ("Make child of “…”"); a dragged child's group shows a "Move out of group" strip under it.
  • Groups. A question, document or post can list others under its card, one level deep. Children are indented so their glyphs line up with the parent's text, and the parent's title ends with the count, such as " (2)". The pill's chevron collapses a group; a collapsed group shows running, failed and updated counts under its text. Clicking a collapsed group expands and opens it; a single click on the selected, expanded group collapses it after 250ms, and a double-click does not.
  • Spring-loading. Hovering over a collapsed tray or group for 650ms during a drag opens it, so you can place the card precisely.
  • Same-folder drops do nothing. Dropping on the folder a question is already in shows a no-drop cursor, and no toast appears.
  • Undo. Every move to another folder, nesting under another item, and Delete (which moves to the Trash) shows a toast with Undo for 6 seconds. Reordering within a folder doesn't show one.
  • Asking from a folder view. A question asked from a user folder's view is filed in that folder. From Drafts or Archive it goes to Unfiled.
  • Naming folders. New folder and Rename open a small modal dialog over the window, with the field focused. Enter or the primary button confirms; Esc, Cancel, or a click outside closes it.
  • Move to. Use a card's "…" menu to open "Move to". It lists every folder, marks the current one as disabled, and ends with "New folder…", which creates the folder and moves the question in one step.
  • Archive. Archive maps to archivedAt. An archived question is listed only in the Archive tray, newest archived first, by its title alone, and stops sending follow-up notifications. Its menu has Unarchive research and no group actions or Rename. A running question moved to Archive keeps running, and the toast says so. An open conversation stays open.
  • Drafts. Drafts is a new system folder. Save draft in the composer's … menu puts a question there; the unused GlobalDraft type already describes a drafts surface. It accepts any question, sent or not, like any other folder. Sending a draft from Drafts moves it to Unfiled. There is no Undo here, because the question has already been sent.
  • User folders. These restore the client-side folders removed in d51a8d4, and should be stored in the backend that was kept. Names must be unique, ignoring case, and "Home" and "Unfiled" are reserved. Deleting a folder needs confirmation, and its questions move to Unfiled. Drafts and Archive can't be renamed or deleted.

Sidebar strip and traffic lights

  • Pin the lights. Set trafficLightPosition so the lights are vertically centred in a 44px header row, which every column header shares. Start from {"x": 16, "y": 16} and adjust on a real device. Today the lights sit in a 30px strip above the sidebar header.
  • Reserve a zone for the lights. With the full sidebar, the zone at the window's top-left is 116px × 44px: it covers the lights (x 16–68) and the sidebar toggle beside them (x 84–110).
  • Keep the lights with the icon strip. The strip's top cell is an empty 44px title-bar row under the lights, with the rule below it; the strip's right border starts below that row, so the column headers' rule runs on across it.
  • The toggle stays put. The collapse button (full sidebar) and the expand button (strip) are the same 26px button at x 84, beside the lights. Collapsing leaves the pointer over the expand button. ⇧⌘G toggles the strip.
  • Inset whatever is under the zone. With the full sidebar no column starts under the zone. With the strip the feed header starts 64px after the strip's edge, and its "Home" title is hidden, since the strip marks Home.
  • Drag region. The whole 44px header row of every column is a drag region (data-tauri-drag-region on the bar, not on its buttons), and double-clicking it zooms the window. Today only the sidebar can be dragged.
  • The strip replaces collapsing to 0px. It keeps navigation and folder drop targets one click away. Icons show tooltips on hover. User folders show the first letter of their name; the tooltip gives the full name, so two folders may share a letter.
  • Automatic strip. Below 900px of window width the sidebar becomes the strip automatically, without changing the saved preference. The expand button beside the lights is disabled there, and its tooltip says why.
  • No hover peek. A hover peek of the full sidebar over the strip was rejected. During a drag it would open whenever the pointer crossed the strip, and the strip icons already act as drop targets.

Groups

The app has no stars or promoted children. Home instead lists one item under another as a group, one level deep (ResearchFolderState.parents, researchGroups.ts). Groups are made by dragging a card onto the middle of another, or with Make child of… in the card's menu, and undone with Move out of group. A group's children are full feed cards, collapsed or expanded with the chevron in the parent's pill. Archive lists every item on its own.

Open questions
